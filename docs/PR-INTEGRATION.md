## Evidence boundaries and gaps

The October 8 handoff also lists accepted raw 8/16-bit buffer handling and runtime formats. The local checkpoint records those integrations and their tests, but the reviewed files inspected for this inventory did not establish their exact upstream PR numbers. They are therefore not assigned PR links here pending a source-to-PR provenance record. The local EXEC workaround also overlaps already merged [PR #1264](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/1264); this is a separately identified upstream equivalent, not a claim that #1264 was fully ported into the older stack.

The local formatted-BDA-span experiment and PR #6921 are explicitly excluded: the former was archived off after a small measured gain, and the latter was rejected as inapplicable to the Linux guest arena. PR #1265 remains a local adaptation, not an upstream contribution by the local project.
